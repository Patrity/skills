The opinions

Five opinions, and the bundle that holds each one up.

They are mine and they are separable. Disagree with one, skip its bundle or its answer, and nothing else breaks.

01

Rules carry the direction, skills carry the how-to

A rule is a glob and a constraint that loads on its own. A skill is the procedure, and it has to be invoked. Splitting them is what keeps CLAUDE.md short enough that Claude still reads it. /skill/nuxt

02

Hooks fail closed

A line in CLAUDE.md is a suggestion. A PreToolUse hook is not. Delete a tracked hook script and the wiring exits 2 rather than waving the edit through. /skill/quality-hooks

03

Docs come in three tiers, and one of them is tested

The wiki is the tier that rots. A registry names every system, and the test fails on a page nobody registered, so drift shows up red in the commit that caused it. /skill/docs-discipline

04

UI work is proven in a real browser

Typecheck, lint and unit tests all pass on a component that never mounted. Snapshot, act on refs, assert with eval, read a screenshot. Then call it done. /skill/browser-testing

05

Memory and process are opt-in

The two habits most likely to be wrong for your project are questions, not defaults. full process is brainstorm, spec, plan, TDD, then a two-stage review; none is also on the list. /skill/iterative-spec-design
